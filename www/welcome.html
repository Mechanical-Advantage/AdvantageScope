mur-voir

Telemetry dashboard for Melbourne University Motorsport.

Open data

  • Open a log with File › Open Log(s)..., or drag a WPILOG, RLOG or CSV file onto the window.
  • Connect to MUR Telemetry or NetworkTables 4 with File › Connect….

Explore

  • Signals appear in the sidebar. Drag a field onto a tab's source list to plot or visualise it.
  • All tabs share one timeline. Click to select a time, scroll to zoom and drag to pan.
  • Add tabs with the + button: Line Graph, 2D/3D Field, Table, Console, Statistics, Points, Metadata and Video.
  • Save and share layouts with File › Export Layout....

Field coordinates

Poses use one right-handed frame: origin at the field centre, +X forward, +Y left, +Z up.
