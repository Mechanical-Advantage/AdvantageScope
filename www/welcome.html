mur-voir

Telemetry dashboard for Melbourne University Motorsport.

  1. Connect. Press Connect… (or Ctrl/Cmd+K) and enter the base-station address. The pill at the top right shows the link, the car and signal freshness.

  2. Drag a field. Signals appear in the sidebar. Drag one onto the Line Graph's left or right axis (signals with different units go on different axes). Scroll to zoom and drag to pan once it is plotting.
  3. Add a tab. Press + for another Line Graph, Laps, Track Map, 3D view, Table or Statistics tab. All tabs share one timeline.

Tip: use View › Toggle Health Strip for pinned fault tiles, and Trackside Layout for pit-wall use. Offline logs open with File › Open Log(s)….
